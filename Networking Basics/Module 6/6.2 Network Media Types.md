# **Network Media – Summary**

Network media provides the **connectivity and path** that data travels through in a network.  
Modern networks use three primary types of media:

- **Wireless (through the air, no physical cable)**
    
- **Copper cabling**
    
- **Fiber optic cabling**
    

---

## **1. Copper: Twisted Pair (UTP)**

### **Unshielded Twisted Pair (UTP)**

- Most common Ethernet cable type.
    
- Contains **8 wires**, arranged in **4 color-coded pairs**.
    ![UTP](/Imagesn/56.jpg)
- Used with **RJ45 connectors**.
    ![RJ45](/Imagesn/57.jpg)
- Connects devices such as **network cards, switches, routers**.
    
- Must follow wiring standards to work properly.
    

### **Key Characteristics**

- Very common in LANs.
    
- Inexpensive and easy to install.
    
- Susceptible to electromagnetic interference (EMI).
    

---

## **2. Copper: Coaxial Cable**

### **Coaxial Properties**

- Contains a **single solid copper core**.
    
- Surrounded by:
    
    - **Plastic insulation**
        
    - **Metal mesh shielding** (protects against EMI)
        
![Copper Coaxial Cable](/Imagesn/54.jpg)
### **Connectors**

- **BNC connector**
    ![BNC Connector](/Imagesn/58.jpg)
- **F-type screw-on connector**
![F Type Screw On Connector](/Imagesn/59.jpg)
    

### **Common Uses**

- Cable TV systems.
    
- Older Ethernet networks (legacy).
    

---

## **3. Fiber Optic Cable**

### **How It Works**

- Made of **glass or plastic**.
    
- Light travels through the core and reflects along an outer layer (cladding).
    
- Carries **light signals**, not electrical signals.
    
![Fiber Optic Cable](/Imagesn/55.jpg)
### **Key Advantages**

- **Immune to electromagnetic interference (EMI)**.
    
- Supports **long-distance transmission** (miles).
    
- Significantly **higher speeds** compared to copper.
    
- More flexible applications due to multiple fiber types and light sources.
    

### **Common Uses**

- Long-distance communication.
    
- High-speed backbone links.
    
- Connections between switches within buildings (short-range fiber).
